What is the Society for Nature & Cognition?

The Society for Nature & Cognition (SNAC) is an affiliate meeting to the Annual Meeting of the Psychonomic Society. SNAC aims to bring together and integrate the rapidly expanding body of empirical reasearch exploring how nature influences attention, emotion, and other core cognitive processes. SNAC will serve as a platform for scholarly exchange and the dissemination of cutting-edge work on the cognitive effects of nature exposure and environmental context.

The inaugural SNAC meeting took place in 2025, with the theme of "New Pathways for Nature and Cognition." It was designed to spotlight emerging perspectives, challenge prevailing paradigms, and inspire innovative directiosn in the field. For an overview of the meeting, please see the Flyer page, which shows the full schedule of the 2025 symposium.

SNAC will be back as a full-day affiliate meeting for Psychonomics 2027 in New Orleans. For 2026, we are presenting a symposium as part of the main Psychonomics conference. The abstracts for this symposium are shown on the Program page.
